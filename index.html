Procedure: Writing Alt Text for Informative Images
A focused CBT module with increasing difficulty
Step 1 of 11

Objective

Given an informative image in webpage context, you will apply a 5-step procedure to write alt text that communicates the image’s essential meaning.

Condition: given an image and its surrounding context
Performance: apply the procedure to write or choose effective alt text
Criteria: score at least 4 out of 5 correctly

Scope of This Module

This module focuses only on informative images.

Informative images add meaning that helps the user understand the page. If the image were removed, some useful information would be lost.

This tutorial does not teach decorative, functional, or complex image procedures as separate categories. In this module, the learner practices one procedure: identifying the essential meaning of an informative image and writing alt text that matches the context.

The 5-Step Procedure

  1. Look at the image.
  2. Identify what information the image adds.
  3. Check what the surrounding text already explains.
  4. Write alt text that captures the essential meaning.
  5. Remove extra details and redundant wording.
Key rule: good alt text for informative images communicates meaning, not every visible detail.

Demonstration 1: Easier Example

Bad vs Good Alt Text

Bad alt text
“Smiling woman holding a clipboard while several people sit around a table in an office”

Why it is weak:

  • Focuses on appearance instead of purpose
  • Includes extra details that do not help the user
  • Misses the main meaning of the image

Apply the procedure:

  1. Look at the image.
  2. The image adds the idea of someone presenting information to a group.
  3. The surrounding text already provides general meeting context.
  4. Write the essential meaning.
  5. Remove extra details about smiling, clothing, and furniture.
Good alt text
“Presenter sharing project information with a team during a meeting”

Demonstration 2: Harder Example

Bad vs Good Alt Text

Bad alt text
“Chart with numbers, years, campus labels, and enrollment information shown in multiple sections”

Why it is weak:

  • Too vague to communicate the main message
  • Lists chart parts instead of summarizing meaning
  • Does not tell the user what trend matters most

Apply the procedure:

  1. Look at the image.
  2. The chart adds information about enrollment growth over time.
  3. The surrounding article already explains the general topic, so the alt text should summarize the main trend.
  4. Write the essential meaning.
  5. Remove unnecessary mentions of layout, colors, and every label.
Good alt text
“Chart showing steady increase in RIT enrollment from 2019 to 2023”

In this module, I begin by looking at the image and its surrounding context.

Then I check what the surrounding text already explains so I do not repeat unnecessary details.
